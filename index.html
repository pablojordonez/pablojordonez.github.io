Economics Specialist, Inter-American Development Bank

I am an economist working on environmental and development topics. My research studies land use and deforestation, the health effects of air pollution, and machine learning for predicting environmental outcomes.

At the Inter-American Development Bank I work on impact evaluation and cost-benefit analysis of development projects. I hold a PhD in Agricultural and Applied Economics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
